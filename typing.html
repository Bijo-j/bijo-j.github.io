How this test works

When you click "Start Test", a random paragraph will appear. Simply start typing it exactly as you see it. The timer starts as soon as you press your first key.

WPM stands for Words Per Minute. The average person types at about 40 WPM. Professional typists and programmers usually type between 65 and 90 WPM. Try to get your accuracy as close to 100% as possible!

Frequently asked questions

What is a good typing speed?

40 WPM is average. 60 WPM is above average and good enough for most office jobs. 80+ WPM is considered professional.

Does accuracy matter?

Yes! If you type 100 words per minute but make 20 mistakes, your actual speed is much lower. This test tracks your exact WPM.

Why is the text so random?

Random text prevents you from memorizing the paragraph and forces you to actually read and type, giving you a true score.
