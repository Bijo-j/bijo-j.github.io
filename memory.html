How this memory test works

When you click Start, one of the colors will flash. After it finishes flashing, click that same color. If you get it right, the game will add a second color to the sequence.

You must repeat the entire sequence from the beginning. The sequence gets longer and faster every level. The average person can remember about 5 to 7 colors in a row. Test your brain and see how you compare!

Frequently asked questions

Is this a good brain exercise?

Yes! This game tests your visual short-term memory and concentration. It is similar to the classic game "Simon".

What is a good score?

Reaching level 5 is average. Reaching level 8 or higher puts your short-term memory in the top tier.

Does this page store my scores?

Your best score is saved temporarily in your browser so you can try to beat it. We do not store your data on any server.
