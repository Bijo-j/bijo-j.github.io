How this test works

When you click "Start", the box turns red. Wait for it to turn green, and click immediately. The time between the green color appearing and your click is your reaction time.

The average human reaction time is around 250 milliseconds (ms). Professional esports players and race car drivers often score below 200 ms. If you click too early (while it is still red), the test resets and you have to try again.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good reaction time?

Anything under 250 ms is above average. Under 200 ms is excellent. Under 150 ms is extremely rare.

Why is my reaction time slower on mobile?

Touchscreens have a slight input delay compared to a computer mouse. For the most accurate score, use a desktop or laptop.

Does this page store my scores?

Your best score is saved temporarily in your browser so you can try to beat it. We do not store your data on any server.
