Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 50% of 100 = (50 × 100) ÷ 100 = 50.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the total and multiply by 100. For example, 25 ÷ 200 × 100 = 12.5%.

Does this page store my numbers?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser.
